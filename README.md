# 🌾 Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

**Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis** is a data analytics project that analyzes agricultural performance across different **crops, seasons, and farming conditions**.

The project uses Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques to identify seasonal patterns, compare crop performance, study relationships between environmental factors and yield, and evaluate profitability.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze agricultural performance across different seasons.
- Compare crop yield, production, revenue, and profit.
- Identify the most and least profitable crop-season combinations.
- Study relationships between environmental factors and crop yield.
- Analyze resource usage and water efficiency.
- Identify important patterns and variations in agricultural performance.
- Provide data-driven insights and recommendations.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **4,000 agricultural records** with **28 attributes** covering:

- Crop and season information
- Farm area
- Rainfall and temperature
- Humidity and sunlight
- Soil conditions
- Fertilizer and pesticide usage
- Irrigation methods
- Crop yield and production
- Market price
- Cost, revenue, and profit
- Water usage and efficiency
- Disease and pest risk

**Dataset file:** `seasonal_agriculture_performance_dataset.csv`

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Exploration
- Dataset structure and dimensions
- Descriptive statistics
- Data types and missing values
- Categorical and numerical feature analysis

### 2. Data Cleaning
- Missing-value identification
- Data validity checks
- Outlier analysis
- Data consistency verification

### 3. Seasonal Analysis
Performance was compared across:
- **Kharif**
- **Rabi**
- **Zaid**

Metrics analyzed include production, revenue, profit, and yield.

### 4. Crop Analysis
The performance of major crops was analyzed, including:
- Rice
- Wheat
- Maize
- Cotton
- Sugarcane
- Chilli
- Groundnut
- Pulses

### 5. Correlation Analysis
Relationships between crop yield and factors such as rainfall, temperature, soil moisture, water usage, and other agricultural variables were examined.

### 6. Profitability Analysis
Crop-season combinations were compared to identify the most and least profitable combinations.

---

## 📈 Key Findings

- **Kharif** recorded the highest average production and revenue among the three seasons.
- **Sugarcane** showed the highest average yield and revenue among the analyzed crops.
- **Sugarcane during Kharif** was the most profitable crop-season combination.
- **Rice during Zaid** recorded the lowest average profit.
- Environmental variables such as rainfall, temperature, and soil moisture showed **very weak linear correlation with yield** in this dataset.
- Profitability varied considerably across different crop-season combinations.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Focus on crop-season combinations with consistently higher profitability.
- Improve water management and irrigation efficiency.
- Consider seasonal conditions when planning crop selection.
- Monitor production costs and market prices to improve profitability.
- Use data-driven analysis to support agricultural planning and resource allocation.
